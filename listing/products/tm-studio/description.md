Tm Studio lets Qlik dashboard authors design cards, lists, infographics and data scenes with their own HTML and CSS. Start with 22 chart, card and list types or four infographic briefings, then adapt the template to your fields and story.

Choose Native, Harbor, Signal or Margin independently of the layout, and share a style through an app variable. Add selections, tooltips and paging with template helpers, ask an AI assistant for a template, or enable a trusted author script in a sealed frame. Tested on Qlik Cloud with Chrome.
